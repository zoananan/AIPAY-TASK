He graduated from [[Princeton University]] in 1986 with a degree in engineering. He worked on [[Wall Street]] in a variety of related fields from 1986 to early 1994. He founded Amazon in mid-1994 on a road trip from New York City to [[Seattle]]. The company began as an online bookstore and expanded to a variety of other e-commerce products and services, including [[Streaming media|video and audio streaming]], [[cloud computing]], and [[artificial intelligence]]. It is the world's largest online sales company, the [[List of largest Internet companies|largest Internet company by revenue]], and the largest provider of [[virtual assistant]]s and cloud infrastructure services through its [[Amazon Web Services]] branch. Bezos founded the [[aerospace manufacturer]] and [[sub-orbital spaceflight]] services company [[Blue Origin]] in 2000, and he flew into space on [[Blue Origin NS-16]] in 2021. He purchased the major American newspaper ''[[The Washington Post]]'' in 2013 and manages many other investments through his [[venture capital]] firm, [[Bezos Expeditions]].
